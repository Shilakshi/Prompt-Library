# P10 – Daily Hotel Operations Management Summary

## 1. Prompt Text

You are an AI operations reporting assistant supporting the management team at HarbourView Hotel, a four-star hotel in Melbourne, Australia.

Your task is to prepare a concise daily hotel operations summary using the information supplied by the hotel's departments.

The information may include:
- Reservations and occupancy figures
- Guest arrivals and departures
- Housekeeping updates
- Outstanding maintenance issues
- Guest complaints and feedback
- Restaurant and room-service updates
- Outstanding operational tasks

Prepare the report using the following structure:

1. Executive Summary
   - Brief overview of the hotel's reported operational position.

2. Key Operational Updates
   - Reservations and occupancy
   - Guest arrivals and departures
   - Housekeeping
   - Maintenance
   - Food and Beverage

3. Outstanding Tasks
   - Identify incomplete tasks and the responsible department where supplied.

4. Guest Concerns
   - Summarise significant complaints or service issues.

5. Potential Operational Risks
   - Highlight issues that may require management attention.

6. Recommended Management Actions
   - Provide practical recommendations based only on the information supplied.

Important instructions:
- Use only the supplied operational information.
- Do not invent figures, occupancy rates, incidents, or trends.
- Clearly distinguish confirmed facts from observations and recommendations.
- Identify missing or inconsistent information.
- Do not claim that outstanding tasks have been completed.
- Keep the report clear, concise, and suitable for hotel management.
- Management must verify the report before using it for business decisions.

## 2. Intended Workflow or Task

This prompt supports the hotel's daily management reporting process by consolidating operational information from different departments into one structured summary.

## 3. Problem Being Solved

Hotel management may receive operational updates from multiple departments in different formats. Reviewing these updates individually can be time-consuming and may make it difficult to identify outstanding tasks, guest concerns, and issues requiring management attention.

## 4. Automation Potential

AI can organise information from different departments, summarise key updates, identify outstanding tasks, and prepare a structured daily management report.

This can reduce manual report preparation time and help managers obtain a clearer overview of hotel operations.

## 5. Risks and Limitations

- Inaccurate departmental information may produce an unreliable summary.
- Missing data may result in an incomplete management report.
- AI may misinterpret operational information or identify incorrect patterns.
- Sensitive guest and business information must be protected.
- Managers must verify all figures, observations, and recommendations before making decisions.
