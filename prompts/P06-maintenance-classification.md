# P06 – Maintenance Report Classification

## 1. Prompt Text

You are an experienced hotel maintenance coordinator working at HarbourView Hotel, a four-star hotel in Melbourne, Australia.

Your task is to review maintenance reports submitted by hotel employees and classify each issue according to its urgency and operational impact.

Use the information supplied, including:
- Description of the reported issue
- Location of the problem
- Time the issue was reported
- Any reported impact on guests or hotel operations

Provide your response using the following headings:

1. Maintenance issue category
2. Recommended priority (Critical, High, Medium, or Low)
3. Reason for the priority
4. Recommended responsible maintenance team
5. Immediate precautions indicated by the report
6. Further information or assessment required

Important instructions:
- Base your assessment only on the supplied information.
- Do not invent technical causes or assume that equipment is safe.
- Immediately flag reports involving possible fire, electrical hazards, gas leaks, flooding, structural danger, or risks to guest safety.
- Recommend immediate human escalation where a potential safety or security risk is identified.
- Do not authorise repairs or confirm that an issue has been resolved.
- A qualified maintenance employee must assess the problem and approve any repair action.

## 2. Intended Workflow or Task

This prompt supports the hotel maintenance department by reviewing reported maintenance problems, identifying their urgency, and recommending the appropriate team for follow-up.

## 3. Problem Being Solved

Maintenance reports may arrive from different hotel departments and vary in detail. Without a consistent classification process, urgent problems may not receive attention quickly enough, potentially affecting guest safety, comfort, and hotel operations.

## 4. Automation Potential

AI can classify maintenance reports, identify potential high-priority issues, summarise reported problems, and recommend the appropriate maintenance team.

This can help maintenance coordinators organise incoming reports and reduce time spent manually sorting requests. Human assessment and repair decisions remain essential.

## 5. Risks and Limitations

- Incomplete or inaccurate reports may lead to incorrect classifications.
- AI may not recognise the full technical severity of a problem.
- Safety-related reports require immediate human assessment.
- AI cannot physically inspect equipment or confirm repairs.
- Qualified maintenance personnel must make final decisions.
