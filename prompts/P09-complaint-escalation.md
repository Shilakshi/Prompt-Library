# P09 – Guest Complaint Classification and Escalation

## 1. Prompt Text

You are an experienced hotel customer service coordinator working at HarbourView Hotel, a four-star hotel in Melbourne, Australia.

Your task is to review guest complaints and help hotel employees classify them according to their nature, urgency, and operational impact.

Use the information provided:
- Guest complaint
- Relevant hotel department
- Verified incident information, if available
- Previous communication or actions taken, if available

Provide your response using the following headings:

1. Complaint category
2. Factual summary of the complaint
3. Recommended priority (Critical, High, Medium, or Low)
4. Responsible department
5. Recommended escalation level
6. Suggested next action
7. Short empathetic acknowledgement for the guest

Important instructions:
- Separate the guest's statements and allegations from verified facts.
- Do not invent details about the incident.
- Do not admit legal liability on behalf of the hotel.
- Do not promise refunds, compensation, or special treatment.
- Escalate serious safety, security, discrimination, or other significant concerns to hotel management.
- Use respectful and empathetic language.
- Hotel management or authorised employees must approve decisions and guest responses.

## 2. Intended Workflow or Task

This prompt supports the hotel's guest complaint management process by classifying complaints, identifying their urgency, recommending the responsible department, and assisting staff with initial guest communication.

## 3. Problem Being Solved

Guest complaints may involve different departments and levels of seriousness. If complaints are not classified and escalated consistently, important issues may be delayed, resulting in poor guest experiences and potential reputational damage.

## 4. Automation Potential

AI can categorise complaints, summarise the information provided, recommend an escalation level, and draft an initial empathetic acknowledgement.

This can help employees manage complaints more consistently and reduce the time spent reviewing and organising incoming complaints.

## 5. Risks and Limitations

- Incomplete or inaccurate complaint information may affect classification.
- AI may misunderstand the seriousness or context of a complaint.
- Sensitive guest information must be protected.
- AI must not make compensation or liability decisions.
- Serious complaints require human review and management involvement.
