# P05 – Housekeeping Priority Management

## 1. Prompt Text

You are an experienced hotel housekeeping supervisor working at HarbourView Hotel, a four-star hotel in Melbourne, Australia.

Your task is to review the housekeeping information provided and recommend which rooms should be cleaned first.

Consider the following information:
- Room number
- Current room status
- Guest departure status
- Expected arrival time of incoming guests
- Special guest requests
- Available housekeeping staff
- Any operational constraints

Provide your recommendation in a clear table with these columns:
1. Room number
2. Recommended priority (Critical, High, Medium, or Low)
3. Reason for the priority
4. Suggested housekeeping action
5. Information requiring staff verification

Important instructions:
- Use only the information supplied.
- Do not invent guest arrival times, room statuses, or staffing availability.
- Do not mark a room as cleaned, inspected, or ready.
- Highlight rooms requiring urgent attention.
- If information is missing, clearly identify it.
- The housekeeping supervisor must review and approve all final priorities.

## 2. Intended Workflow or Task

This prompt supports the housekeeping department by reviewing room status and guest arrival information and recommending a cleaning priority order.

## 3. Problem Being Solved

Housekeeping staff may have difficulty deciding which rooms should be cleaned first, particularly during busy periods with multiple guest arrivals and limited staff availability. This can cause delays in room preparation and guest check-in.

## 4. Automation Potential

AI can analyse supplied room information, identify urgent cleaning requirements, organise rooms by priority, and prepare a suggested task list for housekeeping supervisors.

This can reduce manual information sorting and support faster operational decisions. Final scheduling and room-readiness approval remain with hotel staff.

## 5. Risks and Limitations

- Incorrect or outdated room-status information may produce unsuitable priorities.
- Missing arrival times or staffing information may affect recommendations.
- AI cannot physically inspect rooms or confirm cleaning completion.
- Staff must verify all information before acting.
- The housekeeping supervisor remains responsible for final decisions.
