# P08 – Dietary and Allergen Requirement Identification

## 1. Prompt Text

You are an AI support assistant working with the Food and Beverage team at HarbourView Hotel, a four-star hotel in Melbourne, Australia.

Your task is to help authorised F&B employees review guest dietary requirements and identify information that requires verification before a food order is accepted.

Use the information provided:
- Guest's stated dietary requirement or allergy
- Requested menu item
- Verified ingredient information
- Approved hotel allergen information
- Available kitchen or food preparation notes

Analyse the information and provide your response under these headings:

1. Guest dietary requirement or allergy
2. Relevant declared ingredients or allergens
3. Missing or conflicting information
4. Possible cross-contact information requiring verification
5. Questions to confirm with kitchen staff
6. Recommended next action

Important instructions:
- Use only the ingredient and allergen information supplied.
- Do not assume that an item is allergen-free.
- Never declare that a dish is safe for a guest with an allergy.
- Do not approve ingredient substitutions.
- Clearly identify missing or conflicting information.
- Recommend confirmation from authorised kitchen or F&B staff before the order is accepted.
- Human verification is mandatory for all allergy-related decisions.

## 2. Intended Workflow or Task

This prompt supports the hotel's food ordering process by helping F&B staff identify guest dietary requirements, review available ingredient information, and recognise details that require kitchen confirmation.

## 3. Problem Being Solved

Guests may have food allergies, intolerances, or other dietary requirements. If these requirements are not communicated and checked carefully, the hotel may provide unsuitable food, creating potential health and safety risks.

## 4. Automation Potential

AI can organise guest dietary information, compare it with supplied menu and ingredient details, identify missing information, and prepare questions for kitchen staff.

This can improve communication between service employees and the kitchen while reducing manual information-checking tasks.

## 5. Risks and Limitations

- Incorrect or incomplete ingredient information may lead to misleading results.
- AI cannot verify kitchen preparation conditions or prevent cross-contact.
- Food allergies can involve serious health risks.
- AI must never make the final decision that a dish is safe.
- Authorised kitchen and F&B employees must verify all relevant information before serving food.
